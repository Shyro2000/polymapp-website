Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the PolymApp Android and iOS app and to polymapp.ch, including account and company management. It explains which personal data we process when providing these services.

1. Data controller

PolymApp – Fabian Schatzmann
Solistr. 52, 8180 Bülach, Switzerland
info@polymapp.ch

2. User account and app data

For sign-in, synchronisation and access to features, we process login credentials, email address, user ID, account settings and access entitlements. Personal settings, customisations, machines and tool lists are associated with your account when you use the relevant storage features.

We store device identifiers to manage trial access and prevent repeated use of trials. When someone signs up through a referral, the referred account and its feature activations are associated with the referring user.

The camera is used for local text recognition, without storing recordings or transmitting image or text content to PolymApp or third parties.

We use Google Analytics for Firebase in the Android and iOS app to analyse the app's reach and usage and improve our services. This involves processing and transmitting to Google usage events, such as first opens and sessions, app and device information, technical identifiers and approximate geographic information. The reports show, among other things, active users and frequency of use.

3. Company accounts and shared content

To support collaboration within a company, we store the company name, owner association, memberships, roles and invitations. Members or their company provide email addresses and, where applicable, names, departments, employment status and internal notes.

Company machines, tool lists and maintenance data are accessible to authorised members according to their role. Maintenance entries may contain the date and time, user ID, email address and name of the person performing the work. Administrative member data and internal notes are restricted to the owner and company administrators. Imported private machines become available to authorised members in the company area.

The company is responsible for the employee data and operational content it records. Where we provide this content on its behalf, we act as a data processor. PolymApp remains responsible for account operation, security and its own billing.

4. Subscriptions and billing

Individual app subscriptions are handled through Google Play or the Apple App Store. These providers process the necessary subscription and payment data under their privacy notices. PolymApp uses subscription status to enable access.

Company subscriptions are managed directly by PolymApp. We process the plan, number of employee seats, subscription term, access status, contact person, billing address, billing email address, invoices, payment status and plan credits. Billing details are collected when a company is created, including during the free setup phase.

Within the company, financial data is restricted to the owner; company administrators can see access status and capacity. The PolymApp operator uses the necessary company and contact details for support and billing. Entitlements and plan charges are determined partly automatically. You can request a review by a person via email.

5. Website and contact

Website visits generate technical access data, particularly the IP address, page requested, time of access, and browser and device information. This data is used to provide the website, maintain security and troubleshoot errors. When you register through account management, ipapi.co determines your country from your IP address; the country code is stored in your user profile.

The website uses local storage mechanisms for sign-in, settings and referral attribution. With “Stay signed in” enabled, your login persists after you close the browser; otherwise, it is limited to the session or tab. You can sign out and delete website data in your browser. Security services may use additional cookies or similar storage mechanisms.

When you contact us, we process your email address, message and delivery information to respond. The contact form sends your details to us through FormSubmit.

6. Service providers and processing abroad

The following providers receive the data necessary to deliver their services:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud for authentication, storage and server-side functions; App Check and reCAPTCHA to prevent abuse using technical connection, device and interaction data.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase for statistical analysis of app usage.
  • IONOS for website hosting, access logs and email.
  • ipapi.co (Kloudend, Inc.) to determine the country at registration.
  • FormSubmit to forward contact enquiries.
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store to handle individual app subscriptions.

The Firestore database uses the European multi-region eur3, with locations in Belgium, the Netherlands and Finland. Other services also process data outside Switzerland: Firebase Authentication processes data in the USA; the Cloud Functions we use run in Belgium. Analytics data may also be processed outside Switzerland. The provider of ipapi.co is based in the USA.

Further disclosures may take place where necessary to meet legal obligations or enforce legal claims.

7. Retention and deletion

There is currently no regular automatic deletion of stored account and company data. Invoices and accounting records subject to statutory retention requirements must generally be retained for ten years from the end of the financial year.

Account deletion removes the authentication account and personal user profile, including its associated subordinate data. You may first need to leave your company or transfer ownership. Shared company and maintenance entries, separate billing records and necessary supporting records are not automatically deleted as a result.

For trial device identifiers, the link to the account is removed; the identifier initially remains stored. Analytics is subject to the retention rules configured in the Analytics property. These do not cover all aggregated reports. Technical logs and backups are also subject to the respective providers' retention rules.

8. Legal bases and your rights

The Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection applies in particular. Where the GDPR applies, we process data to perform contracts, comply with legal obligations or pursue legitimate interests in operation, security and responding to enquiries. Consent, where required, can be withdrawn with effect for the future.

Under applicable law, you may request access, rectification and deletion and, where applicable, the provision or transfer of your data, restriction of processing, or exercise your right to object. Please write to info@polymapp.ch. Where your company is responsible for the data, we involve the company. Legal obligations may prevent deletion.

You may contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or the competent data protection supervisory authority.

We update this policy when our data processing changes. The current version is available on the website.
