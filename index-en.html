What is PolymApp?

PolymApp is the all-in-one app for CNC specialists - developed from hands-on experience by a professional polymechanic.
In modern manufacturing, time is the decisive factor. PolymApp was created to bridge the digital gap between the classic reference book and the high-efficiency CNC machine. It combines all essential technical references and calculators into a single application: ISO tolerances, thread dimensions, undercuts, material data, surface specifications, and much more - always at your fingertips, even without an internet connection directly on the shop floor.

Top 5 Reasons to Choose PolymApp

1. Intelligent Camera-Based Text Recognition: Stop entering tolerances manually. Simply scan a dimension on your technical drawing, such as 30 H7, and PolymApp instantly displays the corresponding limit dimensions. This saves valuable time and prevents transcription errors.

Simply scan a dimension on the drawing and instantly receive the limit dimensions.

2. Intelligent Pre-Tolerance Calculator: Are your components coated or surface-treated after machining? PolymApp automatically calculates the required pre-tolerances while taking coating thicknesses, such as galvanizing or anodizing, into account. This ensures that the final dimension is correct after coating.

Enter the coating thickness and instantly receive the correct pre-tolerances.

4. Offline-First for the Workshop: Mobile reception is often limited in production halls. Therefore, all core PolymApp functions are available offline, directly where they are needed at the machine.

5. By Professionals for Professionals: PolymApp is not a theoretical IT project. It is a practical, intuitive and fast tool developed specifically for the daily requirements of polymechanics and CNC specialists.

Detailed Functions for Your Production

Mechanics & Tolerances

  • Tolerance Calculator: ISO limit dimensions for shafts and bores.
  • Thread Calculator: Tap-drill diameter, pitch diameter and tolerances for all common thread types.
  • Undercuts: Quick access to standard dimensions according to DIN 509 and DIN 76.
  • Surface Finish: Quickly understand roughness values such as Ra and Rz and their corresponding symbols.
  • Spirit Level: A simple digital spirit level for quickly checking and aligning workpieces, fixtures or other surfaces.

Calculations & Materials

  • Cutting Data: Efficiently calculate spindle speed, feed rate and machining time.
  • Material Database: Physical properties, standard designations, suitable cutting data and much more for over 400 materials.
  • Hardness Comparison: Quickly and accurately convert values such as HRC, HB, HV or N/mm² – including a machinability assessment.
  • Geometry: Integrated calculations for triangles, circles and polygons.
  • G/M Codes: Command lists for Fanuc, Heidenhain and Siemens.

CNC Troubleshooting

  • Guided question-and-answer troubleshooting: PolymApp guides you step by step through troubleshooting a CNC machine. First select the machining process: milling or turning.
  • Select the main problem: Tool wear, surface-finish problem, noise problem, dimensional accuracy, chip problem or temperature problem.
  • Select the tool: End mills, indexable milling cutters, side and face cutters, chamfer mills, T-slot cutters, radius cutters, turning tools, grooving tools, parting tools, drills, deep-hole drills, indexable drills, exchangeable-head drills, reamers, taps, thread mills, forming taps, threading inserts and special tools such as knurling and boring tools.
  • Narrow down the problem: For a surface-finish problem, for example, you can select scoring or vibration marks, a dull surface, defects in specific areas or an uneven sheen.
  • Select the symptoms: Available symptoms include a rough surface, interrupted cutting, feed marks, a tapered face, chatter marks, a whistling or squealing tool, a slipping workpiece or built-up edge formation.
  • Diagnosis and corrective action: PolymApp displays the most likely main cause, possible additional causes and specific actions that can be taken to solve the problem.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Which thread types are supported?
The app covers an extremely wide range and provides all relevant dimensions for:
• Metric threads: Standard threads (M), fine threads (MF) as well as Helicoil (metric & fine). In addition, user-defined fine threads can be calculated individually.
• Inch & US Threads: UNC, UNF, UNEF, UNJC, UNJF, and UN-6 to UN-32 series.
• Pipe & British Standards: G (Pipe thread), BSW, BSF, and BA.
• Specialty Threads: Trapezoidal, ACME, Stub ACME, as well as NPT and NPTF.
You will find the correct dimensions for almost any requirement instantly.

Does PolymApp support special coatings?
Yes, the integrated pre-tolerance calculator allows you to enter specific coating thicknesses. With just one click, you get the exact manufacturing dimensions you need to maintain before surface treatment (e.g., anodizing or galvanizing) to ensure the final fit is perfect.

Can PolymApp support Quality Assurance (QA) tasks?
Absolutely. By quickly looking up limit dimensions and surface finishes directly at the inspection station, target-actual comparisons can be carried out much more efficiently. The fit calculator and detailed roughness specifications help to instantly resolve ambiguities during drawing reviews.

Is PolymApp suitable for apprentices, other professions, or hobbyists?
Definitely! PolymApp is an excellent learning tool for apprentice polymechanics to understand the logic behind ISO tolerances. Furthermore, design engineers, production planners, and ambitious hobbyists or makers benefit from professional data to implement projects with industrial precision.
