Delete company

Checking deletion requirements.

All company machines, tool lists, maintenance plans and maintenance history, G/M code lists, member data and invitations will be permanently deleted. Your personal account, private machines and their maintenance data will be retained.

Transfer ownership

The selected user becomes the new owner. Your own role will automatically change to admin.

Before deleting your account, you must transfer ownership if other members are present.

Leave company

Your private machines and their maintenance data will remain unchanged. After leaving, you will no longer have access to company machines, tools or their maintenance data.

Review billing details

As the owner, please review the billing address. The company name entered when creating the company has been prefilled in the “Company” field and can be changed or cleared.

After ownership is transferred, the new owner must review and save these details again.
